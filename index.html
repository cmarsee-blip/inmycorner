Got Questions?

We've Got Answers.

Everything you need to know about InMyCorner — for parents, kids, and athlete volunteers.

Still have questions? Contact us →
How does the matching process work?
+

After a parent creates a profile for their child — including their age, grade, and favorite sports — our platform matches them with a verified athlete volunteer who plays the same sport. We consider age-appropriateness and availability to make the best possible pairing.

Is InMyCorner free for families?
+

Yes! Signing up and connecting your child with an athlete penpal is completely free for families. We believe every kid deserves access to a mentor regardless of financial situation.

Can I see the messages my child exchanges?
+

Absolutely. Parents have full visibility into all conversations. You can read every message exchanged between your child and their athlete penpal at any time through the parent dashboard. Transparency is a core part of how we operate.

How much time does volunteering require?
+

We designed this with busy athletes in mind. Most penpals exchange messages once every one to two weeks. There's no strict requirement — you set your own pace. Even a short, thoughtful message can make a huge difference in a kid's week.

Who is eligible to volunteer as an athlete?
+

Any active collegiate (D1, D2, D3, NAIA, NJCAA) or professional athlete in any sport can apply. All volunteers go through a brief screening and background check process before being matched with a child.

How do you keep kids safe on the platform?
+

Safety is our top priority. All athlete volunteers are background checked before joining. All messages are sent through our monitored platform — no personal contact info is ever exchanged. Parents can view all messages at any time, and our moderation team reviews flagged content within 24 hours.

What happens if a match isn't working out?
+

No problem at all. Parents or athletes can request a rematch at any time, no questions asked. We want every connection to feel natural and positive — we'll work to find a better fit as quickly as possible.

What sports are currently available for matching?
+

We currently support over 24 sports including baseball, basketball, football, soccer, swimming, volleyball, track & field, softball, tennis, golf, wrestling, gymnastics, and more. If your child's sport isn't listed yet, join our waitlist and we'll notify you when it's available.
